## 1. Aim of the game

Build your own Bundesliga Fantasy team and score points based on the real-life performances of Bundesliga players.

The better your players perform on a Bundesliga matchday, the more Fantasy points your team earns. Compete against other managers in **classic leagues** or **head-to-head leagues**, join public leagues or create private leagues with friends.

## 2. Your team

### Squad and budget

Your Fantasy team consists of **15 players**:

- 2 goalkeepers
- 4 to 6 defenders
- 4 to 6 midfielders
- 2 to 4 forwards

At the start of the season, you receive an automatically generated squad with a total value of **150 million**.

Your team must always stay within your available budget. Your budget cannot go below zero.

Your squad value can change throughout the season as player market values are updated after each matchday.

If you join during the season, you will also receive an automatically generated squad. Your starting budget will be adjusted based on the average squad value of active managers, so you can still start on a competitive level during the season.

Player positions are set before the start of the season and remain unchanged throughout the season.

### Team name

Your team is automatically given a team name. You can change it at any time in your user profile.

### Club limit

You can have a maximum of **3 players from the same Bundesliga club** in your squad.

## 3. Lineup and matchday

### Formation

You can change your formation until kick-off of the first match of a matchday.

Once the matchday has started, your formation can no longer be changed for that matchday.

### Starting lineup and Top 11 mode

**Top 11 mode** is always active. This means your matchday score is based on the **11 highest-scoring players in your squad who can fit into your selected formation**.

Example: If you play a formation with two forwards, only two forwards can count towards your Top 11.

Make sure your selected formation fits your squad. If a position slot in your formation cannot be filled, no player will score in that slot. In this case, fewer than 11 players may count towards your matchday score.

### Star players

You can select **3 star players** per matchday:

- one star player in defence
- one star player in midfield
- one star player in attack

Star players score **1.5 times their points**.

If one of your selected star players does not make your Top 11, the star player bonus is not automatically transferred to another player.

### Automatic saving

Changes to your team are saved automatically.

### Joining during an active matchday

If you join while a matchday is already in progress, you will receive an automatically generated squad.

The points scored by that squad will count towards your total for the current matchday, even if some matches have already been played.

For leagues, the league start date applies:

- If the league has already started on the current matchday, you will score points in that league.
- If the league starts on a future matchday, your score in that league will only count from that matchday onwards.
- Newcomer leagues for new managers always start with the next relevant matchday.

### Points

Your players earn points based on real Bundesliga events. You can find the full points breakdown in the “Scoring” tab.

Matchday points may be updated until **12:00 noon Berlin time** on the day after the final match of the matchday if official match data changes afterwards.

### Postponed or abandoned matches

If a match is postponed or abandoned, the affected players receive their average points score. If an affected player was selected as a Star player, that score is multiplied by 1.5.

If a match is postponed or abandoned on Matchday 1, there is no individual average points score yet. In this case, the affected players receive the average points score of all players in their position who played at least 70 minutes on that matchday.

## 4. Transfers and market values

### Transfers

Before your first scored matchday, you can make unlimited transfers.

After the season has started, you have **5 transfers** available between two matchdays.

A transfer is possible as long as all squad rules are met:

- 15 players in your squad
- 2 goalkeepers
- 4 to 6 defenders
- 4 to 6 midfielders
- 2 to 4 forwards
- maximum of 3 players from the same Bundesliga club
- sufficient budget

You can replace an outfield player with a player from the same or a different outfield position, as long as your squad still meets all position requirements afterwards.

For goalkeepers, the rule is different: If you sell a goalkeeper, you must also buy another goalkeeper, as your squad must always include exactly 2 goalkeepers.

### Reversing transfers

You can reverse transfers until kick-off of the first match of a matchday.

If you reset your transfers, you may need to adjust your starting lineup and star players again.

### Breaks with unlimited transfers

During selected breaks in the season, unlimited transfers are available.

For the **2026/27** season, this applies during the following periods:

- **1st international break:** between Matchdays 4 and 5 (21 September 2026 to 8 October 2026)
- **2nd international break:** between Matchdays 9 and 10 (9 November 2026 to 19 November 2026)
- **Winter break:** between Matchdays 14 and 15 (21 December 2026 to 7 January 2027)
- **3rd international break:** between Matchdays 27 and 28 (22 March 2027 to 1 April 2027)
- **UEFA break:** between Matchdays 31 and 32 (26 April 2027 to 6 May 2027)

### Dynamic Market Values

Player market values are updated automatically after every matchday.

Each player has a target value based on their recent performances and established performance level. If the target value is higher than their current market value, their value increases. If it is lower, their value decreases.

The following factors apply:

- The more a player's performance differs from their expected level, the more significantly their market value may change.
- Playing time also matters: brief appearances generally result in smaller changes.
- Players who do not feature in a match retain their current market value.

You can track a player's market value development in the “Market Value Trend” section of their player profile.

### Daily Bonus

Collect your **Daily Bonus** every day to increase your available transfer budget.

The Daily Bonus runs in a 7-day streak. If you collect it on consecutive days, your daily reward increases.

Once you have collected all 7 days in the streak, the streak starts again from the beginning. If you break your streak, it also resets.

## 5. Leagues

### Official leagues

You automatically take part in the **Bundesliga Fantasy League**.

The **2nd Chance League** also starts on **Matchday 15**. In the 2026/27 season, it begins on **8 January 2027**. All managers automatically take part in the 2nd Chance League as well.

Within a league, you can view both the overall standings and the standings for individual matchdays.

### Newcomer leagues

If you join after the season has started, you will automatically be assigned to a newcomer league.

This league starts with the next relevant matchday. Only managers who joined at the same time compete against each other in that league.

Example: If you create your Fantasy team after the start of Matchday 2 and before the start of Matchday 3, you will enter the Matchday 3 newcomer league.

### Public leagues

You can join public leagues. First, you select a league format. You are then automatically assigned to a suitable public league.

Public leagues usually have **18 participants**. In head-to-head Knockout Mode, they usually have **16 participants**.

The fewer matchdays remain in the season, the smaller the league size may be.

Per matchday, you can join one public league per format:

- one classic league
- one head-to-head league in League Mode
- one head-to-head league in Knockout Mode
- one head-to-head league in Playoff Mode

You can only join an automatically assigned public league before that league starts. Once the league has started, no more managers can join.

You cannot leave this type of public league.

### Private leagues

You can create your own private leagues or join private leagues created by other managers.

Private leagues can only be accessed via an invite code or an invite link.

When creating a private league, you define:

- league name
- league format
- starting matchday

Private classic leagues can have an unlimited number of participants.

For private head-to-head leagues, the admin sets a maximum number of participants. This setting cannot be changed after the league has been created.

**Important note:** Choose a realistic maximum number of participants for private head-to-head leagues. Empty slots can lead to byes. A bye counts as an automatic win for the manager drawn against that empty slot.

### Club leagues

You can join official club leagues and compete with other fans of a Bundesliga club.

Club leagues are publicly accessible and have no fixed participant limit.

## 6. League formats

### Classic Mode

In Classic Mode, your scored team earns Fantasy points on every matchday.

Your matchday score is based on your Top 11 within your selected formation. The points from all scored matchdays are added together.

The manager with the most total points at the end of the league wins.

Classic leagues can be official, public or private. Official leagues are always played in Classic Mode.

### Head-to-head modes

In head-to-head leagues, you compete directly against other managers. Depending on the mode, you either play for points in a table or for progression in knockout duels.

In League Mode and in the league phase of Playoff Mode, the following scoring applies:

- Win: 3 points
- Draw: 1 point
- Loss: 0 points

In Knockout Mode and in the playoff rounds of Playoff Mode, the manager with more Fantasy points in the direct duel advances.

### League Mode

In League Mode, you play a direct duel against another manager on every matchday.

The league runs until the end of Matchday 34.

Fixtures are based on the number of participants and the number of matchdays played in that league. This means you may face the same manager more than once and may not play every other manager the same number of times.

The manager with the most head-to-head points at the end of the season wins the league.

### Knockout Mode

In Knockout Mode, you play direct duels in a tournament bracket.

The first-round pairings are drawn. After that, the rest of the bracket is fixed.

In each round, you face another manager:

- The winner advances.
- The loser is eliminated.

Each round is decided on one matchday. In leagues with two-legged ties, each round — except for the final — takes place across two matchdays.

The number of participants and the number of matchdays in that league determine the number of tournament rounds and the duration of the league.

### Playoff Mode

In Playoff Mode, you first play a league phase, followed by a playoff phase.

During the league phase, you compete in direct duels against other managers. The best managers from the league phase qualify for the playoffs.

The length of the league phase depends on the number of participants, the number of playoff rounds and the number of matchdays played in that league.

The league phase and playoff phase together run until the end of Matchday 34.

In the playoffs, the highest-ranked qualified manager faces the lowest-ranked qualified manager. The second-highest ranked qualified manager faces the second-lowest ranked qualified manager, and so on.

### Byes

If you do not have an opponent on a matchday or in a round, you automatically receive a bye.

A bye counts as a win.

### Joining and leaving head-to-head leagues

For automatically assigned public head-to-head leagues:

- You can only join before the league starts.
- Once the league has started, no more managers can join.
- You cannot leave the league.

For private head-to-head leagues in League Mode:

- You can join at any time as long as the league is not full.
- You can leave at any time.

For private head-to-head leagues in Knockout Mode:

- You can only join or leave before the tournament starts.
- Once the first knockout round has started, no more managers can join or leave.

For private head-to-head leagues in Playoff Mode:

- You can join or leave until the playoff phase starts.
- Once the playoff phase has started, no more managers can join or leave.

### Ties in head-to-head tables

If two or more managers are tied on points in a head-to-head table, the ranking is decided in the following order:

1. higher number of wins
2. higher total Fantasy score
3. a fixed internal rule to ensure a clear ranking

This rule is used, for example, to determine which managers qualify for the playoffs in Playoff Mode.

### Ties in knockout duels

In knockout rounds, exactly one manager advances from each duel.

The winner is the manager who scores more Fantasy points in that duel.

If both managers score the same number of Fantasy points, the winner is decided in the following order:

1. higher star player score
2. higher total squad value
3. draw

## 7. Prizes

You can win prizes in selected competitions.

To see which leagues award prizes and what those prizes are, go to the **Prizes** section of the Fantasy Manager. Open the information area via the **info icon** and select “Prizes”.
