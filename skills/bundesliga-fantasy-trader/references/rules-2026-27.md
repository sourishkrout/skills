# Bundesliga Fantasy Manager 2026/27 rules

## Source and authority

This reference summarizes the official article **“Bundesliga Fantasy Manager 2026/27: Rules, player values and unlimited transfers,”** published 22 July 2026:

<https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/fantasy-manager-2026-27-rules-player-values-unlimited-transfers-38316>

It also incorporates the clearer in-app `4. Transfers and market values` rules supplied from the Bundesliga app on 16 September 2026. Use this as the 2026/27 baseline. The app is authoritative for current rules, player positions, prices, sell values, budget, squad state, and transfer legality. If a newer dated official publication or current app rule changes a rule, follow the newer/current source, disclose the change, and flag this reference for updating.

## Squad construction

- Initial budget: EUR 150 million.
- Squad size: 15 players.
- Required composition: exactly 2 goalkeepers, 4 to 6 defenders, 4 to 6 midfielders, and 2 to 4 forwards.
- Club limit: no more than 3 players from one club.
- Player positions are assigned at the start of the season and do not change during it.
- Player values and the squad's available budget can change during the season.

## Matchweek lineup and stars

- Formation, lineup, and star selections lock at kickoff of the matchweek's first fixture.
- Top 11 mode automatically counts the 11 highest-scoring squad players permitted by the selected formation.
- Select 3 stars: 1 defender, 1 midfielder, and 1 forward.
- Each star scores 1.5 times their normal points.
- If a selected star does not make the Top 11, the bonus does not transfer to another player.

## Transfers

- Transfers are unlimited before the user's first scored matchday.
- After the season begins, 5 transfers are available between two matchdays.
- A valid transfer package must remain within budget, preserve 15 players and the required positional composition, and respect the 3-per-club limit.
- An outfield player may be replaced by a player from the same or a different outfield position when the resulting squad still meets every positional limit.
- Selling a goalkeeper requires buying another goalkeeper because the squad must always contain exactly 2 goalkeepers.
- Transfers may be reversed until kickoff of the matchweek's first fixture.
- Reversing transfers may require the lineup and star selections to be set again.

## Unlimited-transfer periods

The ordinary 5-transfer limit is removed during these 2026/27 windows:

- 21 September to 8 October, between Matchdays 4 and 5.
- 9 November to 19 November, between Matchdays 9 and 10.
- 21 December to 7 January, between Matchdays 14 and 15.
- 22 March to 1 April, between Matchdays 27 and 28.
- 26 April to 6 May, between Matchdays 31 and 32.

Verify the year-spanning dates and live transfer allowance in the app before relying on an unlimited window.

## Market values and daily bonus

- Market values move toward a target based on recent performance and established performance level.
- Larger differences from expected performance can cause larger value changes.
- Playing time affects value movement; brief appearances generally produce smaller changes.
- A player who does not appear retains their current market value.
- The app's Market Value Trend shows each player's development.
- A daily bonus increases the available transfer budget through a 7-day streak. Completing or breaking the streak resets it.

## Points and disrupted fixtures

- The app's Scoring tab contains the full points breakdown.
- Matchweek points can be corrected until midday CET on the day after the final fixture when official match data changes.
- For a postponed or abandoned match, affected players receive their season-average points; a starred player's average is multiplied by 1.5.
- On Matchday 1, when no individual average exists, an affected player receives the average score of players in the same position who played at least 70 minutes that matchday.
